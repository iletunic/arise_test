Introduction to Open Access and Open Science Principles

Building on the previously developed course “Introduction to Open Access and Open Science with focus on Open Data”.

Objectives of the course

The course is an introduction to the topic of Open Science, open data and open infrastructures. The course further conveys knowledge about how to access scientific literature, manage data openly and publish results.

It sets a base for the following courses which will be delivered after this course:

  • FAIR data and Data and software management.
  • Introduction to EOSC
  • Introduction to European Research Infrastructures and Open access to RI by ERICs.

The course focuses on ideas and procedures to implement Open Science principles in research.

ARISE fellows will become familiar with the structures supporting Open Science at EMBL and how to work aligned with the policies. They will get exposed to the practices used by other organisations.

Learning outcomes defined using actionable verbs as in Bloom’s Taxonomy

Following this course, participants will be able to:

  1. Recall contact points for obtaining information on Open Science at EMBL
  2. Explain the differences between ways of how research was traditionally handled and Open Science
  3. Explain why Open Science is important in research
  4. Name and apply the FAIR Principles
  5. Apply the EMBL Open Science Policy
  6. Use the knowledge to select the right option to publish research results openly
  7. Describe the difference between Gold Open Access and Green Access and chose accordingly

RI specific competencies to be addressed by this training

Competencies selected from the ARISE competency framework.

Training should contribute to the development of following knowledge, skills and attitudes. Approximate level of competency to be developed by the course is indicated for each competency selected.

Knowledge: - Knows principles of Open Science and how they apply to data, publications, software and operation of RIs. (medium) - Knows how to publish Open access scientific publications (advanced) - Recognises the importance of organisational values such as open science and environmental responsibility. (advanced) - Understands the FAIR guidelines and principles for data management. (basic) - Recognizes the role of Research infrastructures in the Open Science mission (basic) - Understands the basic ethical principles underlying research in the research infrastructure (RI) field. (medium)

Skills: - Identifies relevant experts about Open Science policies and practices (advanced) - Analyses research outputs competently, taking into account all available information. (medium) - Uses and aligns research data management plans and policies with FAIR principles. (basic)

Attitudes: - Demonstrates commitment to continuous learning and self-awareness related to organisational values. (medium) - Avoids manipulation of information. (advanced) - Promotes and supports the implementation of FAIR data principles within the organisation. (advanced)

Course timing and structure

When delivering a course, trainer will structure it in 4 phases, that follow this order:

  1. What is Open Science
  2. What is Open Access to publications, data, software, research infrastructures
  3. Challenges in Open Science
  4. Open Science application - example from EMBL and from King’s College
