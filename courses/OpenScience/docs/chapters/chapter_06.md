# 6. Further ideas for exercises

!!! question "Exercise 5: The Open Science “Pillar” Scavenger Hunt"

    **Duration:** 20 minutes

    **Format:** Small Breakout Rooms (4-5 people per room)

    **Goal:** To connect real-world (EMBL) resources with which trainees are familiar to the UNESCO Pillars of Open Science.

    **The Setup:** Assign each group one "Pillar" from the UNESCO definition:

    -   Group 1: Open Scientific Knowledge (Publications/Data)
    -   Group 2: Open Science Infrastructures
    -   Group 3: Open Engagement of Societal Actors / Science Communication

    **The Task:** Using the provided materials (or quick web search of the mentioned links), find two specific examples of how EMBL or King’s College London supports your assigned pillar.

    -   *Example for Group 2:* Mentioning the Bio-ImageArchive or Euro-BioImaging ERIC as a way to provide physical/virtual access to instrumentation.
    -   *Example for Group 1:* Mentioning the Rights Retention Strategy at EMBL or the EMBL DMA tool.

    **Activity Flow:**

    -   2 min: Instructions and breakout room assignment.
    -   10 min: Group work in breakout rooms.
    -   8 min: Rapid-fire reporting (1 minute per group) where one spokesperson shares their "finds" with the main room.
